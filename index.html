What NorthSyte catches

Pattern rules for the things that follow a format, and an in-browser AI model for the things that don't.

Credentials & secrets

API keys for AWS, OpenAI and Stripe, GitHub and Slack tokens, private keys, passwords, bearer tokens, database connection strings, internal URLs and IP addresses.

Financial

Credit card numbers verified with the Luhn checksum, security codes, IBANs, US routing numbers, SWIFT/BIC codes and CRA business numbers.

Government & personal IDs

Canadian Social Insurance Numbers with a Luhn check, US Social Security Numbers, Canadian and US passports, and driver's licences across select provinces and states.

Healthcare

Provincial health card numbers across Ontario, BC, Quebec, Alberta, Newfoundland and the Northwest Territories, plus US provider IDs, DEA numbers and Medicare IDs.

Personal & contact info

Email addresses, phone numbers and Canadian postal codes, alongside the names and addresses that usually travel with them.

AI layer

People, organizations & places

A model running inside the browser reads meaning, not formats, so it catches the names and street addresses that no pattern rule could ever match.
